# HOCKIA member interviews

Short conversations that tell us what members actually do and need. The founder runs them; the agent prepares the guide, the recruiting message and the summary. The agent never invents interviews, answers or quotes.

## Before: plan
1. **Decision to inform.** One sentence: "We'll decide <X> (e.g. what Home leads with for clubs)."
2. **Unknowns.** List the hypotheses from `docs/product/user-research.md` this round tests.
3. **Who.** 5–8 people per round, mixed by role; for a role-specific question, 5 of that role. Active and lapsed members both help. **Adults only (18+).** No admin-blocked members.
4. **Format.** 20–30 minutes, video call or voice note, in the member's language (Spanish/English). Recording only with explicit consent.
5. **No incentives that cost money** unless the founder decides.

## Recruiting message (adapt; founder sends)
"Hi <first name>, I'm building HOCKIA. Could I ask you about how you <find clubs / recruit players / …> — 20 minutes, no preparation? It's to make the app more useful, not to sell anything. Happy to do it whenever suits you."

## Consent and privacy
- At the start: what it's for, how notes are kept, that they can skip any question or stop. Ask before recording.
- Notes and recordings stay outside the public repo and are deleted when summarised (founder's decision on retention).
- Summaries are anonymous: role, country/region and level at most. No names, clubs that identify someone, or verbatim quotes that identify them.

## The guide (Mom Test rules)
Ask about the **last time** something happened, not opinions or futures. Don't pitch, don't show designs until the end, don't ask "would you use…".
1. Context: "Tell me about your hockey right now — where you play/coach, what's next for you."
2. Last real episode: "The last time you <looked for a club / needed a player / changed team>, what did you do, step by step?" → "Who helped? What was hardest? What did it cost you (time, money, missed chance)?"
3. Current tools: "Where do you hear about <roles / players / moves>? What do you check most often?" (WhatsApp, agents, Instagram, federations, HOCKIA.)
4. HOCKIA in their week: "When did you last open HOCKIA? What were you looking for? Did you find it?" → "What do you look at first? What do you skip?"
5. Signals of value: "What would make you open it every week?" only after 1–4; treat as a weak signal.
6. Optional, last: show 2–3 Home directions and ask what they'd do first on each (observe, don't defend).
Follow-ups: "Why?", "What happened next?", "Can you show me?" Avoid leading words ("easy", "useful", "love").

## After: summarise (per interview, then per round)
```
Interview <n> — role, region, level, active|lapsed, date
Did (past behaviour, facts):
Pains (their words paraphrased, anonymised):
Workarounds / alternatives:
What they look for in HOCKIA:
Surprises:
Hypotheses supported / contradicted: H1 …, H2 …
```
Round summary → add to `docs/product/user-research.md`: dated evidence bullets (aggregated, "4 of 6 clubs …" only if those counts are real), updated hypotheses, research-log row.

## Never
Invent participants, answers, counts or quotes; interview minors; store identifiable notes in the repo; treat "I would use it" as evidence.
