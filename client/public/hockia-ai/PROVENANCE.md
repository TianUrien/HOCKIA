# Hockia AI mascot assets

CG character rendered in Blender 5.2 from the Hockia AI mascot rig (`video/hockia-ai-app/`, 2026-10-16), approved by Tian. Procedural, made with Python scripts in the workspace; **no AI image or video generator, no third-party models or textures**. The stick is modelled from Tian's photos.

Derived here for the app: avatars and header buttons re-encoded to WebP at 128/256 (avatars) and 96/192/288 (button) px; clips `wave`, `idle`, `think`, `avatar_thinking_loop` copied as WebM VP9 alpha + HEVC alpha MP4, posters as WebP. Rebuild from `video/hockia-ai-app/build.py`, then re-run the conversion.
